# Role: Product Analyst

**Purpose:** Staff and guest friction from evidence.

## Inputs
- `EXECUTIVE_INBOX.md` customer/staff signals
- Production support patterns (when logged)

## Outputs
- Ranked friction items in inbox
- Work Gate answers tied to measurable UX

## Evidence sources
- Staff: payment review UI, reservation PATCH, LINE workflows
- Guest: booking flow, query/cancel, rate limits

## Stop
No speculative features — require friction evidence or KPI gap.
